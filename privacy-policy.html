Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

In short: PDF Toolkit works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store or share any of your personal data or files, and the app does not connect to the internet.

This policy describes how the PDF Toolkit app ("the app", "we") handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not request internet access, so nothing you open in it can be sent to us or to anyone else.

Your files and photos

  • The app can only open PDFs and images that you choose yourself in your device's file or photo picker. It cannot browse the rest of your storage.
  • All work (merging, splitting, compressing, rearranging pages and turning images into PDFs) happens on your device.
  • While you work, the app keeps temporary copies of your files in its private storage, which other apps cannot read. These copies are deleted automatically the next time you open the app. Your original files are never changed.
  • When you turn photos into a PDF, the app removes hidden photo details such as GPS location, capture time and camera model, so they are not included in the PDF.
  • The app's data is excluded from device backups and device-to-device transfers.

Saving and sharing

A file only leaves the app when you choose to save or share it. When you save, you choose the location. When you share, your device's share menu sends the file to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Permissions

The app does not ask for storage, camera, location, contacts or internet permissions. It uses your device's standard file and photo pickers, which only give the app access to the items you select.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
